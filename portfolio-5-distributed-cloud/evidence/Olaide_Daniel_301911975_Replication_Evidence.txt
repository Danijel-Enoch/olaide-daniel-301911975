# NOTE: statement_timeout cannot interrupt this wait, which is why the
# write is observed rather than timed out. The commit is already durable
# on the primary; what is outstanding is the standby's acknowledgement,
# and PostgreSQL will not abandon it - cancelling the client would leave
# a commit that is locally durable but unacknowledged to the application.
# Reads are completely unaffected:

# This is the CAP trade-off in one experiment. With its synchronous
