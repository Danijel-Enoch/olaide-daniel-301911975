K3  DAILY DEBIT LIMIT - the write-skew problem from Portfolio 3 D5,
    solved by atomicity instead of by SERIALIZABLE
======================================================================
# D5 showed two REPEATABLE READ transactions each checking a 50,000.00
# daily limit, both passing, and together breaching it. Here the check
# and the increment are ONE atomic server-side script, so no two
# clients can interleave between them.
# three 30,000.00 debit attempts against a 50,000.00 daily limit:
  attempt 1 -> 1|30000|ALLOWED
  attempt 2 -> 0|30000|LIMIT_EXCEEDED
  attempt 3 -> 0|30000|LIMIT_EXCEEDED
total recorded: 30000
# exactly one 30,000.00 debit was admitted. The limit held without any
# isolation level, retry loop or 40001 error to handle.
